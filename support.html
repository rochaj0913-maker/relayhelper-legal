Frequently Asked Questions

The app is crashing or not loading. What do I do?
Try these steps in order:
  1. Force-close the app and reopen it
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Restart your iPhone
  4. Delete and reinstall the app from the App Store (your subscription will be restored automatically)
If the problem persists, contact us at support@relayhelper.app.
How do I subscribe to Relay Helper Pro?
Open the app and tap the "Subscribe — $1.99/week" button on the paywall screen. You will be charged $1.99/week automatically through your Apple ID. Cancel anytime in Settings.
How do I cancel my subscription?
To cancel:
  1. Open your iPhone Settings
  2. Tap your Apple ID (your name at the top)
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap Relay Helper
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period. You will not be charged again after cancellation.
I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall. How do I restore?
On the paywall screen, scroll down and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to purchase. If it still doesn't work, force-close the app and try again.
Will I get a refund if I cancel?
Relay Helper does not offer refunds for partial subscription periods. You can cancel at any time, and access continues until the end of the paid period. For refund requests, contact Apple Support directly through the App Store.
The AI gave me wrong information. What should I do?
AI responses are informational only. Always verify critical decisions with your dispatcher or carrier's official policy. If you notice consistently wrong answers, please email us so we can improve the system prompt.
Can I use Relay Helper in Spanish?
Yes! Tap the language toggle button (EN/ES) in the top right corner of the chat screen to switch between English and Spanish. The AI will respond in whichever language you write in.

No. Relay Helper is an independent third-party app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon.com, Inc. "Amazon Relay" is a trademark of Amazon.com, Inc.
