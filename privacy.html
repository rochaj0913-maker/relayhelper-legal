Privacy Policy

Relay Helper

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Short version: We collect anonymous chat interactions to improve AI answers for all drivers. We do not collect your name, phone number, location, or any personally identifiable information. No account is required.

1. Information We Collect

Relay Helper collects only the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Chat interactions (anonymous): Questions asked and AI responses received are stored in our database to power the collective learning feature. These are not linked to any user identity.
  • Votes: When you tap thumbs up or thumbs down on an AI response, we record that vote (without any identifier) to improve future answers.
  • App usage (anonymous): Basic analytics such as app opens and feature usage may be collected via Firebase Analytics. This data is aggregated and not tied to individuals.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
  • Carrier account information or load IDs
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)

3. How Data Is Stored

Anonymous chat interactions are stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database service. Data is stored in the United States. Firebase's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Data

  • To provide AI-powered answers to driver questions
  • To improve the quality of AI responses over time through collective learning
  • To understand which features are most useful (analytics only)

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Relay Helper uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API — processes your chat messages to generate AI responses. Messages are sent to Anthropic's servers. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase / Firestore — stores anonymous interaction data. See Firebase Privacy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases. RevenueCat may collect device identifiers for subscription management. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous chat interactions are retained indefinitely to improve AI answer quality. You may request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Relay Helper is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests: support@relayhelper.app
